## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

AIOS is not a healthcare platform.

AIOS is not a financial platform.

AIOS is not an HR platform.

**AIOS is the Universal Economic Intelligence Operating System** - a collection of 15 fundamental computational engines that any enterprise domain can compose to solve complex economic, analytical, and decision-making problems.

Healthcare, Finance, HR, Supply Chain, Legal, ESG, and AI Governance are just **vertical applications** that run on top of these universal engines.

## THE 15 UNIVERSAL ENGINES

### 1. ECONOMIC ENGINE
**Purpose**: Model costs, revenues, value flows, and economic relationships

**Capabilities**:
- Cost attribution and allocation
- Revenue forecasting
- ROI calculation
- Economic value measurement
- Price elasticity modeling
- Market dynamics simulation
- Budget optimization
- Capital allocation

**Domain Applications**:
- Healthcare: Claims cost analysis, PBM spread detection, stop-loss optimization
- Finance: P&L forecasting, margin analysis, capital planning
- HR: Total rewards cost modeling, benefits ROI
- Supply Chain: Landed cost calculation, inventory carrying costs
- Legal: Contract value analysis, litigation cost forecasting

---

### 2. STATISTICAL ENGINE
**Purpose**: Probability distributions, hypothesis testing, statistical inference

**Capabilities**:
- Distribution fitting (normal, lognormal, gamma, Weibull, etc.)
- Hypothesis testing (t-test, chi-square, ANOVA)
- Regression analysis (OLS, GLM, mixed models)
- Credibility weighting
- Confidence intervals
- P-value calculation
- Bayesian inference
- Survival analysis

**Domain Applications**:
- Healthcare: Medical trend credibility, claim frequency analysis
- Finance: Risk-adjusted returns, VaR calculation
- HR: Compensation benchmarking, turnover prediction
- Supply Chain: Demand variability analysis
- ESG: Impact measurement statistical validation

---

### 3. SIMULATION ENGINE
**Purpose**: Monte Carlo simulation, scenario generation, stochastic modeling

**Capabilities**:
- 10,000+ scenario Monte Carlo
- Multi-dimensional uncertainty modeling
- Correlation preservation
- Tail risk analysis
- Extreme event simulation
- Bootstrap resampling
- Agent-based modeling
- Discrete event simulation

**Domain Applications**:
- Healthcare: Claims variability forecasting, stop-loss attachment optimization
- Finance: Portfolio risk simulation, stress testing
- HR: Workforce planning scenarios
- Supply Chain: Inventory stockout risk
- Legal: Litigation outcome probability
