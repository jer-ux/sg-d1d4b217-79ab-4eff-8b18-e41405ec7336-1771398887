## THE END STATE VISION

**Not:** "Software that produces actuarial reports"

**But:** "The operating system for enterprise intelligence"

### What This Looks Like

An organization asks:

> *"What happens to our EBITDA if we switch PBM vendors, adjust our stop-loss corridor, optimize our 401(k) match structure, and refinance our credit facility?"*

**The Platform:**
1. Queries all relevant data sources (claims, financials, workforce, contracts)
2. Activates specialist AI agents (Actuary, CFO, CHRO, Risk Officer)
3. Runs multi-scenario simulations (Monte Carlo, sensitivity analysis)
4. Validates across compliance frameworks (ERISA, SOC 2, GAAP)
5. Quantifies uncertainty intervals (P10, P50, P90 outcomes)
6. Produces board-ready analysis with full evidence provenance
7. Delivers: **"Expected EBITDA impact: +$8.2M (range: +$6.1M to +$11.4M, 87% confidence)"**

**Execution timeframe:** Minutes to hours, not weeks to months.
